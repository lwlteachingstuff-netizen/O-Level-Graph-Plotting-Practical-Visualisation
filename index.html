Scale Rule
Singapore Practical Scale Selection Rules:
  • Use convenient scales: multiples of 1, 2, 5, or 10 for each big square ($2\text{ cm}$).
  • Points must cover ≥ 50% (ideally ≥ 75%) of the graph paper in both dimensions.
  • No. of decimal places: $\frac{1}{2}\text{ smallest division} = \frac{D}{20}$. Round to 1 s.f. to find d.p.

SLAP Master Review & Evaluation

Singapore GCE Science Practical Rubric

Evaluation
S Scale Selection & Grid Area Convenient ≥ 75%

Scale uses multiples of 1, 2, or 5 per 2 cm major square. Plotted points span ≥ 50% (ideally ≥ 75%) of both grid axes. Awkward scales (e.g., multiples of 3, 7) are penalized.

L Line of Best Fit Balanced & Single Stroke

Straight, continuous pencil stroke with 30 cm ruler (no kinks, gaps, or thick overlaps). Points are evenly balanced on both sides of the line, passing through centroid $(\bar{x}, \bar{y})$.

A Axis Labeling & Solidus Quantity / Unit

Axes clearly labeled with standard Solidus notation ($Quantity / Unit$). Scales are marked and numbered every 2 cm (1 big square) with correct decimal places ($D/20$).

P Plotting Accuracy & Crosses × within ½ sm sq

All points plotted with sharp graphite crosses (×) spanning 2 small squares. Plotted coordinate accuracy is verified to within ± 0.5 small square.
